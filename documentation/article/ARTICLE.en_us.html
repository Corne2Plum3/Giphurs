Giphurs is an original typeface created by Corne2Plum3, starting in 2022. It is inspired by néo-grotesque typefaces such as Helvetica and Arial. Its simple, geometric design ensures clarity and legibility while remaining versatile enough to fit a wide variety of contexts and designs. Angled terminals and subtle details, such as the rounded corners on the 't' and '1' or the slight slant of the '!', give the typeface a distinct personality while allowing it to remain serious and functional when needed.

It is a variable font with a weight range from Thin (100) to ExtraBlack (1000), supporting the Latin, Greek, and Cyrillic scripts, as well as hundreds of arrows and mathematical symbols. Italics are also supported. A wide range of alternate glyphs is available, especially for digits, with at least two alternatives for each. Finally, small caps are included, and a version of the font with small caps enabled is also provided.

To contribute, see github.com/Corne2Plum3/Giphurs.
